## Psychology of the Unconscious II.V

To-day there are countless neurotics who are so simply because they do not know how to seek happiness in their own manner.

## Psychology of the Unconscious II.VI

The more a person withdraws from adaptation to reality, and falls into slothful inactivity, the greater becomes his anxiety (cum grano salis), which everywhere besets him at each point as a hindrance upon his path.

The retrospective longing acts like a paralyzing poison upon the energy and enterprise; so that it may well be compared to a poisonous serpent which lies across our path.
